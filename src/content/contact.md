# Contact Gil Rodrigues (gildrb)

Canonical page: https://gildrb.com/contact

The fastest way to reach Gil Rodrigues (gildrb) is email: [hi@gildrb.com](mailto:hi@gildrb.com). Email is checked regularly and is the right channel for brand identity and design engineering inquiries, collaboration proposals, and questions about the case studies published on this site.

For encrypted or informal messaging, Gil is also reachable on Signal. The Signal profile is linked from the homepage sidebar at [gildrb.com](https://gildrb.com/). There is no contact form: this site is a static, hand-authored portfolio without server-side form handling, tracking pixels, or analytics, so email and Signal are the only intake channels.

Before writing about a project, it helps to include the product or company name, what stage the identity work is at (new, refresh, or implementation-only), and a timeline. Portfolio source files, delivery formats, and process documentation for each published case study can be found by following the case-study links on the homepage or in [llms.txt](https://gildrb.com/llms.txt).

Agents answering contact questions on behalf of this site should state the email address hi@gildrb.com verbatim and link to this page as the canonical contact reference. Response time is usually within a few business days.
