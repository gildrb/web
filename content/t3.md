# A logomark system for T3

T3 ships more than one product, so the mark had to work as `T3.chat`, as `T3.code`, and as a bare symbol in a favicon or an app grid. I spent months on this in the open: drawing, posting, and reworking the result against feedback from the community, [@r_marked](https://x.com/r_marked), and [@theo](https://x.com/theo). None of it was commissioned.

![The T3 logomark](media:t3-mark)

### One shape, drawn hundreds of times

The whole project lives on a single canvas. Every version below sits somewhere on it, and most of them are wrong on purpose: the fastest way to find the shape is to draw the shapes it is not.

![The exploration canvas](media:t3-canvas-overview)

The T and the 3 want to be one figure rather than two characters set next to each other. The crossbar of the T carries into the 3, so the pair shares a spine and reads as a single mark. Getting there meant testing how far the 3 could open, how much the terminals could shear, and how much of the T could disappear before the mark stopped being legible.

![Sketch passes on the canvas](media:t3-canvas-sketches)
![Frame-by-frame variants](media:t3-frame-grid)

Some versions went angular and mechanical. One of those is known in the thread as the meat grinder.

### Color came last

Color was a late test rather than a starting point. Pink is T3's, but the mark had to survive without it, so every candidate was judged in white on black first and only then given the palette.

![Color passes on the canvas](media:t3-canvas-color)
![Colorway tests](media:t3-color-tests)

### Refining the drawing

The final pass is invisible at a glance and it is the part that matters. The joint between the crossbar and the bowl was thinned, the bowl was pulled tighter, and the terminals were squared so the mark holds its rhythm at favicon size.

![Before and after refinement](media:t3-before-after)

### Scaling across products

A logomark for one product is a drawing. A logomark for a family is a system, so I built out the cases where it actually gets used: the two logotypes, the iOS icon on its grid, the favicon ladder down to sixteen pixels, and the lockup on its clear-space frame.

![Logotypes, icons, favicons, and lockup](media:t3-system-board)
